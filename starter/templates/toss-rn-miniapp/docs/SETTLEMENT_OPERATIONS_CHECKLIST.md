# Settlement Operations Checklist

## Workspace Setup
- [ ] Business registration is complete if monetization is required.
- [ ] The representative admin can accept the relevant Toss agreements.
- [ ] Settlement info review is approved in the console.

## Settlement Inputs
- [ ] Bank-account copy is prepared and matches the registered account holder exactly.
- [ ] Ad revenue operations owner is assigned.
- [ ] Popbill reverse-invoice approval owner is assigned.

## Ongoing Ops
- [ ] Daily/launch dashboards are checked on `+1 day` for analytics and ad data.
- [ ] Monthly ad settlement is reviewed after the next-month update.
- [ ] Reverse-invoice approval is completed within the required window to avoid payout delays.
