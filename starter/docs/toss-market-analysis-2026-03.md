# Toss Market Analysis 2026-03

Last reviewed: `2026-03-28`

This note summarizes public App-in-Toss signals reviewed on `2026-03-28`. Facts are sourced from official Toss properties. Any interpretation is labeled as inference.

## Current Public Signals
- The public Apps in Toss site currently advertises reach to `29,000,000` cumulative users and cites Toss internal data through the end of July 2025.
- The developer overview currently describes App-in-Toss as a way to expose services to roughly `30 million` cumulative Toss users.
- The same overview says typical launch timing is about `2 to 4 weeks` for games and `2 to 3 months` for non-game services.
- The February 9, 2026 Tossfeed milestone said partner miniapps crossed `1,000`, with average launch pace of about `4.8` new miniapps per day after official launch.
- That same February 9, 2026 milestone said cumulative users exceeded `51 million`, and that `95%` of partners who joined over the prior `10` months were still operating.
- The October 15, 2025 Tossfeed milestone said App-in-Toss crossed `200` partner miniapps within `100` days.
- That same milestone reported about `2.6 million` cumulative users, about `15 million` cumulative pageviews, and about `6.7` minutes of average dwell time.
- The January 30, 2026 webinar recap said Toss analyzed `300+` game datasets and emphasized instant-launch HTML5 services as a strong pattern.
- The Apps in Toss public site currently says service monetization can combine ads, in-app purchase, push, and promotion tooling.

- Toss said `안전집사` acquired `99%` of new customers through App-in-Toss and grew monthly revenue by `40x`.
- Toss said `용사단 키우기` reached `800,000` cumulative users in about `1.5` months after launch inside App-in-Toss.
- Toss publicly highlighted `돌돌디` as a representative game-side success case in the February 9, 2026 `1,000` miniapp milestone coverage.
- Toss said solo-built `코심` was made in `2 weeks` and generated `2.6 million KRW` in revenue during its first week without paid marketing.
- Toss said two-person `디스팟` was made in `5 days` and generated `10 million KRW` in its first month.

## What This Means
- Inference: distribution and instant access are the main structural advantage, so first-release scope should bias toward low-friction entry rather than feature breadth.
- Inference: games are currently the fastest-scaling category because Toss publicly says they account for about `50%` of the miniapp base and repeatedly uses game case studies in milestone communication.
- Inference: non-game apps still have strong upside when they solve a narrow, clear task and do not overbuild before distribution fit is validated.
- Inference: the best first releases in this channel are not broad products. They are single-purpose, immediately understandable surfaces with one strong repeat reason.
